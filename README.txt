CHERRY - phone app (works offline)

1. Put it online (free, 1 minute)
   - On a computer, open https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - Drag the whole "cherry-app" folder onto the page.
   - Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app

2. Install on your phone
   - Android (Chrome): open the link, tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
   - iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

3. Use it
   - Open Cherry from your home screen. After the first open it works with no internet.
   - The same link also works as a normal web page in any browser.

Your data stays on your phone. Use Backup > Export now and then.
To move data from the Claude web version, export there and import here.
